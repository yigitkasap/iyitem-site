Patient Rights and Data Protection

What you should know about your rights and how your personal data is processed.

01 Access to services in general

Every individual who applies to a healthcare institution has the right to benefit from all services, without distinction of language, religion, race or denomination.

02 Information and requesting information

You have the right to learn what healthcare services and facilities are available, and to request, receive a copy of and inspect complete, up-to-date information and documents about the diagnosis and treatment process and about how your treatment is progressing.

03 Choosing and changing the institution and its staff

During the provision of healthcare, every individual has the right to choose and change the doctor, nurse and other healthcare staff responsible for their treatment and care, and — subject to the procedures and conditions laid down in the legislation — to choose and change the healthcare institution.

04 Privacy

You have the right to receive care in an environment that respects your privacy at every stage of the healthcare provided. Unless there is a medical necessity and you have given permission, you have the right not to share private information about yourself and your family life, on whatever grounds.

05 Refusal, suspension and consent

Except where required by law, and with responsibility for any adverse consequences resting with the patient, a patient has the right to refuse treatment that is planned or being administered, or to ask for it to be stopped.

06 Safety

Patients and those accompanying them, such as visitors and companions, have the right to have the necessary measures taken to protect and ensure the safety of their persons and property.

07 Fulfilling religious obligations

You have the right to fulfil your religious obligations freely, without distinction of religion, so long as this does not interfere with treatment.

08 Respect for human values, dignity and comfort

You have the right to receive healthcare with respect, care and attention, in a welcoming, courteous and compassionate environment in which all hygiene requirements are met and noise and other disturbances have been eliminated.

09 Visitors and companions

You have the right to receive visitors within the procedures and principles set by the healthcare facility, and to have a companion present within the limits of the legislation and the facility's capacity, where the physician considers it appropriate.

10 Right of application, complaint and legal action

Without prejudice to the rights of application to the relevant professional bodies and the courts under Law no. 6023, you have the right to submit complaints to the Patient Rights Units within healthcare institutions and, if a complaint cannot be resolved within the institution, to apply to the most senior local administrative authority in the place where the institution is located. Legal rights also include the right of recourse to the courts.
