<p>At İyitem Imaging Centre we scan with a 2022 Siemens Magnetom Vida 3 Tesla MRI system. Thanks to artificial intelligence, sequences can be accelerated up to four times.</p>

<p>The 70 cm wide bore gives claustrophobic patients a more comfortable environment, and you can watch television or listen to music during the scan. When needed, the “Silent MRI” feature reduces the noise considerably. Patients weighing up to 250 kg can be scanned at our centre.</p>

<p>Metal artefact reduction sequences give clearer images in patients with prostheses. Alongside routine MRI we perform specialised examinations such as functional MRI, MR spectroscopy, MR tractography, MR enterography, cardiac MRI, multiparametric prostate and liver MRI, MR neurography and whole-body MRI.</p>
