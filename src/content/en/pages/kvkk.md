<h3>İstanbul Yüksek İhtisas Sağlık Yatırım ve Tic. Ltd. Şti. — Patient Information Notice on the Processing of Personal Data</h3>

<p>As İstanbul Yüksek İhtisas Sağlık Yatırım ve Tic. Ltd. Şti. (“İYİTEM”), in line with the general working principles of a diagnostic centre, making effective use of advanced tools, instruments and equipment, and in accordance with occupational health, workplace safety and environmental protection regulations and with the efficiency and quality requirements of the profession, we perform and assess medical imaging and interventional radiology procedures for the diagnosis and treatment of illness in the human body. In our capacity as the data controller identified below, we obtain and keep confidential your personal data in accordance with Law no. 6698 on the Protection of Personal Data, through our records and notification system, our staff, authorised persons, our website, mobile applications, our social media pages, our physical premises and similar channels, verbally, in writing, visually or electronically. With this document we fulfil our duty to inform and request your explicit consent to the processing of your personal data.</p>

<p>Company name: İstanbul Yüksek İhtisas Sağlık Yatırım ve Tic. Ltd. Şti.<br />MERSİS number: 0481008503600016<br />Address: Dr. Adnan Adıvar Caddesi No:5 Haseki / İSTANBUL<br />Telephone: 0212 632 00 00<br />Fax: 0212 589 19 57<br />E-mail: iyitem@iyitem.com</p>

<p>We process your personal data — name, surname, Turkish ID number, passport number, temporary Turkish ID number, nationality, country, city, place and date of birth, marital status, gender, occupation, insurance card number, workplace registration, patient number, protocol number, address, telephone number, e-mail address, bank account and IBAN number, credit card details, Social Security Institution and private health insurance data, medical reports, diagnostic and examination data, physicians' analyses and comments, appointment details, information relating to sexual life, camera recordings and IP address — for the purposes set out in the <strong>İyitem Personal Data Protection and Processing Policy</strong> (the “Policy”), available at <a href="/media/2022/11/iyitem_KVKK_politikasi.pdf">www.iyitem.com/media/2022/11/iyitem_KVKK_politikasi.pdf</a>, and in accordance with Articles 5 and 6 of the Law, primarily in connection with the imaging and diagnostic centre services we provide.</p>

<p>We protect your personal data by taking all necessary technical and administrative measures for the periods set out in the <strong>Personal Data Retention and Destruction Policy</strong>, also available at that address, and we erase, destroy or anonymise it in the periodic destruction window (6 months) following the end of that period.</p>

<p>Subject to the legislation and to your explicit consent, we may share your personal data domestically — with natural persons or private-law legal entities, business partners, suppliers, authorised public institutions and organisations, authorised physicians, private insurance companies, auditors, consultants, and domestic organisations with which we have a service contract or cooperate — for the purposes determined in accordance with Article 8 of the Law; and abroad, where the conditions set out in Article 9 of the Law are met.</p>

<p>Using your contact details, we send electronic messages — subject to your permission — informing you about the services we provide in connection with our activities and introducing new products and services. We stop sending these at your request.</p>

<p>You may submit requests under Article 11 of the Law, which governs the rights of the data subject, as set out in the Policy, by sending İYİTEM the <strong>Application Form</strong>, details of which are available at <a href="/media/2022/11/iyitem_veri_sahibi_basvuru_formu.pdf">www.iyitem.com/media/2022/11/iyitem_veri_sahibi_basvuru_formu.pdf</a>.</p>
